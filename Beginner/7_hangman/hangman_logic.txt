Here is the logic for the Hangman game.

1. Start
2. Generate a random word.
3. Generate as many blanks as letters in word.
4. Loop
    a. Ask the user to guess a letters
    b. Is the guessed letter in the word?
        i. Yes, Replace the blank with the letter.
            a. Are all the blanks filled.
                i. No? go back to 4.a.
                ii. Yes? go to 5.
        ii. No, Lose a life.
            a. Have they run out of lives?
                i. No? go back to 4.a.
                ii. Yes? go to 5.
5. Game Over!
